## Additional [[House Rules]]
- Crunchy Crits
	- Take the max of the die to be rolled and add that to a normal damage roll. 
	- Example, nat 20 with a maul. 2d6+12+modifiers.
- Exhaustion for Spells
	- You may cast spells in exchange for levels of exhaustion. 
	- Half spell level rounded up. 
	- Level 1 & 2, 1 exhaust. 3 & 4, 2 exhaust. So on. 
- Wild Shapes at rest free
	- When not in combat and can take time, wildshapes do not use wildshape charges. 
	- Rushing does use a charge. 
- Grim Hollow Wounds
	- When downed roll for minor, major, or permanent wounds.
	- J has the table.
